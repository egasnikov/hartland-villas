The Vision

Some homes are built to impress. This one is built for calm.

Step inside and the city falls quiet. Light moves across natural stone, water sits calm in the garden, and tall walls of glass open the home to the outdoors.

Most homes at this level ask you to choose: wait years for a render to become real, or buy a finished villa that looks like every other villa on the street. AVRA refuses the choice. These are finished, architect-designed estates, built to the standard of a private commission, in one of Dubai's most established green communities.

The Concept

Four ideas, drawn from the site

The massing, the materials, the landscaping. Everything traces back to four words.

01

Water

Pools and still water sit at the heart of the plot, so the house mirrors itself and the light stays soft.

02

Landscape

Greenery climbs onto the roof terraces and wraps around the home, so the garden feels part of the architecture, not an afterthought.

03

Natural Materials

Travertine, marble, and timber, used honestly and at scale. A single marble spine runs the full height of the facade.

04

Detail

Backlit stone used like artwork. Dressing rooms lined in leather. The luxury is in the details, not the labels.

The Architecture

Quiet, low, and timeless.

Wide, flat roofs float above walls of glass, lined underneath with warm timber. A single line of marble runs the full height of the facade and ties the design together. There is no unnecessary decoration. The beauty comes from proportion, light and shadow, and honest natural materials.

Interiors

Open, light-filled living.

Living, dining and kitchen flow together and open through glass onto the terrace, the lawn and the pool. Upstairs, every bedroom has its own dressing room, bathroom and terrace.

Questions

Good to know

Where exactly are the villas?+

Sobha Hartland, Nad Al Sheba 1, in Mohammed Bin Rashid City, Dubai. A green, established community minutes from Downtown, with the waterfront, Sobha Hartland Mall, parks and schools all within walking distance.

Who designed them?+

The architecture and interiors are by SL Architects, a Dubai and Moscow bureau that builds private residences. Interiors are specified to a curated set of leading Italian houses including Minotti, Poliform, and Rimadesio.

How many are available?+

One signature villa, AVRA Prima, and seventeen Collection villas. That is the entire release. When they are gone, there is no second phase.

Are they freehold and open to all nationalities?+

Yes. Sobha Hartland is a freehold community, open to buyers of all nationalities.

What are the prices?+

The Collection villas start at AED 25M. AVRA Prima, the signature villa, is AED 36.5M. Each is offered by private appointment; to arrange a viewing, please contact your preferred broker.

Eighteen villas. One release. No second phase.
